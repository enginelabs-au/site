## The problem

Agentbox at MCG held a few hundred people who had asked for a market appraisal in the last 18 months and not listed, a couple of hundred past buyers, and the landlords on the rent roll. Everyone had opted in. Nobody was hearing from the agency unless an agent had a quiet afternoon and did a call-around. Follow-up was sporadic, and sporadic follow-up in real estate is the same as none.

## What I built

Three segments come out of Agentbox each week: past appraisals by month since the appraisal, settled buyers reaching their first anniversary, and landlords approaching a rent review. Before anyone is drafted for, the Engine checks their contact preferences in Agentbox and the unsubscribe list. No consent, no draft.

Drafts are personal in the way a good agent's email is personal. They mention the property, what has sold nearby since according to Pricefinder, and one genuine reason to get in touch. If someone replies asking for an updated appraisal, the agent gets a Realtair proposal pre-filled with the property and comparables so the follow-through is quick. Each morning the agent gets one WhatsApp: today's five drafts, one line each. Y sends them all from the agent's own Gmail, spaced out through the day. N holds them. A number pulls that one out and the Engine asks why, so the next batch is better. Sends are throttled to a sensible daily number so the agency never looks like a mail-out.

Every reply lands in the agent's Gmail and is logged against the contact in Agentbox. The Engine never replies.

## What it deliberately does not do

No cold lists. No scraped data. No text messages without express consent. No sending without that morning's Y. The compliance setup is why this Engine costs more than the [Sales Engine](/lab/mcg-sales-engine-portal-enquiries) and why I would not build it any other way.

## What changed since

The repeat-enquirer rule from the Sales Engine is what prompted this build. Once the agents saw how many people were circling listings without being spoken to, they wanted the same discipline applied to the people already in their database.
